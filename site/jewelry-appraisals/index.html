Questions people ask

What should I bring?

The piece, plus any paperwork you have: receipts, earlier appraisals or diamond grading reports. They help, but they're not required.

How much does an appraisal cost?

It depends on the piece. Call (361) 993-9391 or ask at the counter and we'll tell you before we start.

Can you appraise watches and loose stones?

Yes. We appraise timepieces and loose gemstones as well as finished jewelry.

Is an appraisal the same as an offer to buy?

No. An appraisal estimates value, often the replacement value for insurance. If you're thinking of selling, we can also make you an offer. See we buy gold.

Who does the appraisal?

Malak Afram, a Graduate Gemologist trained by GIA, who also works at our repair bench.
