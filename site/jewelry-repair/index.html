Jewelry is meant to last. Sometimes it needs help.

A clasp gives out, a prong wears thin, a stone works loose. When the piece means something, you want it back looking the way you remember it. Afram's has repaired jewelry for South Texas families since 1978, from everyday chains to rings that have been in the family for generations.

Our repair center is right in the store, so you can bring the piece in, look it over with us at the counter, and know what it needs before any work starts. Not sure it's worth the drive? Text us a photo first.

Questions people ask

Can you fix it while I wait?

It depends on the repair. Some quick fixes happen while you're in the store and others take longer. We'll tell you at the counter before any work starts.

How much will my repair cost?

It depends on the piece and the work. Bring it in, or text a photo to (361) 993-9391, and we'll give you a quote.

Do I need an appointment?

No. Come by during store hours, Tuesday to Saturday, 10 AM to 6 PM.

Will you repair jewelry that wasn't bought at Afram's?

Yes. Bring it in wherever it came from.

Do you fix watches too?

Yes. Batteries, bands and repairs are at the same counter. See watch repair.
