See it on your wrist first.

Buying a watch online means guessing at the size, the weight and how the dial looks in real light. At Afram's you can see the Seiko collection in person, try a few on, and have the bracelet adjusted to fit.

As an authorized Seiko dealer, every Seiko we sell is genuine and comes with Seiko's own warranty. And when it needs a battery or a band adjustment down the road, bring it back to the same counter.

Questions people ask

Is Afram's an authorized Seiko dealer?

Yes. Every Seiko we sell is genuine and comes with Seiko's warranty.

Can I try a watch on before I buy it?

Of course. That's the point of coming in. We'll adjust the bracelet so it fits.

What if the model I want isn't in the case?

Ask us. Call (361) 993-9391 or come in, and we'll look into it for you.

Yes. Batteries, band adjustments and repairs are at the same counter. See watch repair.
