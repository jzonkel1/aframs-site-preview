Every customer, treated like family.

Joseph Malak Afram opened Afram's Jewelers in 1978, and he treated every employee and every customer like family.

His family carries on that legacy of integrity and personal service today. It's why people bring us the bracelet they bought fifty years ago, the ring another shop said was too old to fix, and the band they're upgrading for their thirtieth anniversary.
