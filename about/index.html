Every customer, treated like family.

Joseph Malak Afram opened Afram's Jewelers in 1978. A master jeweler, diamond setter and custom designer, he built the store on careful work and honest dealing, and he treated every employee and every customer like family.

His family carries on that legacy of integrity and personal service today. Malak Afram, a Graduate Gemologist trained by GIA with an engineering degree from Texas A&M, is a skilled bench jeweler and watch repairman, and the store still has a complete repair center behind the counter.

It's why people bring us the bracelet they bought fifty years ago, the ring another shop said was too old to fix, and the band they're upgrading for their thirtieth anniversary.
