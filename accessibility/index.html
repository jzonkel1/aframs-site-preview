Accessibility Statement

Last updated October 24, 2026

Afram's Jewelers wants everyone to be able to use this website, including people who use screen readers, keyboard navigation, voice control or larger text.

Our goal

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA, and we check the site as we make changes to it.

What we've done

The site uses clear headings, descriptive text for images, labeled form fields, visible focus outlines, readable color contrast and layouts that work from small phones to large screens. It can be used with a keyboard alone. If your device is set to reduce motion, the photo slideshow on the home page stays still, and pointing at or tabbing to one of its labels holds that photo.

Content from other companies

Some features come from other companies, including our online catalog, Acima's lease-to-own application, Google Maps and our social media pages. We can't control how accessible they are, but we're happy to help you with anything they cover.

Need help or found a problem?

If any part of this site is hard to use, call or text (361) 993-9391, email aframsjewelers@gmail.com or stop by the store at 1701 Airline Rd, Corpus Christi, TX 78412, Tuesday to Saturday, 10 to 6. We'll help you directly and work to fix the problem. Please tell us the page and what happened.
