Questions people ask

Can every ring be resized?

Most can. A few styles, like bands with stones all the way around, are harder to size. Bring the ring in and we'll tell you what's possible.

How long does ring sizing take?

It depends on the ring and how much it's changing. We'll give you a time at the counter.

My diamond is loose. Should I keep wearing it?

We'd take it off and bring it in. A loose stone can fall out, and tightening it is usually much easier than replacing it.

Do you size rings bought somewhere else?

Yes. Bring it in wherever it came from.

Do I need an appointment?

No. Come by during store hours, Tuesday to Saturday, 10 AM to 6 PM.
