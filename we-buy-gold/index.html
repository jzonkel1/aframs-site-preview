Questions people ask

Do I need an appointment to sell gold?

No. Come in any time during store hours, Tuesday to Saturday, 10 AM to 6 PM.

What should I bring?

Your pieces and a valid photo ID. We're required to record it when we buy.

Will you buy broken jewelry?

Yes. Broken chains, single earrings and scrap gold are exactly what we buy every day.

How is my offer figured?

Gold is valued by its purity and weight against the day's market price, and stones and watches are valued on their own. We'll walk you through the offer at the counter.

How do I get paid?

In cash, on the spot, once you accept the offer.

Do you buy coins?

Our sister store, Capital Coin, buys coins and bullion, in the same building at 1701 Airline Rd.
